This directory contains the excerpt **“The Woman in the Wilderness and the Dispute in Heaven”** from the book *The End of Time*.  
The excerpt explains how a mistranslated Greek verb in Revelation 12 created two incorrect doctrinal positions and the appearance of a “war in heaven” that the Greek text does not describe.

## Summary

The excerpt covers:

- The mistranslation of the Greek verb meaning *shepherd*, not *rule*.  
- How this mistranslation produced two false spiritual positions (“the bull of Baal” and “the plow”).  
- The meaning of *erēmos* — the woman’s solitary, protected location.  
- Why the “war in heaven” is a dispute over meaning, not a physical conflict.  
- How correcting the mistranslation restores the narrative structure of Revelation 12.

This excerpt is part of the larger project to present the text in its original conceptual form, without doctrinal distortions introduced by translation errors.
